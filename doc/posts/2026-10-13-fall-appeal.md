## Meetup group message

Subject: TTN NY fall newsletter, and a new way to support us

The Meetup editor turns the event link into "here"; it adds tracking parameters to the link and keeps deleted links, so check the copied text before sending.

```text
Hi all,

TTN NY sent out its fall newsletter today. It covers three projects from the past year:

- Mind Your Feelings, a public art kiosk on mental health at the Johnson Public Library in Hackensack, NJ. We helped design its electronics and firmware.
- A people-counting trial for the NYC Department of Transportation, which used four LoRaWAN gateways we set up and ran.
- Internet for Python classes at three high schools in western Kenya.

Read it here: https://mailchi.mp/thethings/news-from-the-things-network-new-york-fall-2026

If you'd like the next issue in your inbox, the signup link is on the home page of https://thethings.nyc.

We're an all-volunteer 501(c)(3). Donations now go through Zeffy, which charges us no fees, so all of your gift reaches TTN NY: https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/donate-to-the-things-network-new-york

And you're welcome to come to a meetup. You don't need a technical background. Our next meetup is Thursday, October 22, at Fat Cat Fab Lab. Details here: https://www.meetup.com/the-things-network-nyc-community-meetup/events/316961338/

Best regards,

--Terry
```

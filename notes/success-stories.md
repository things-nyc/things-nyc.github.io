## 1. Mind Your Feelings (Hackensack, NJ)

- **slug:** `mind-your-feelings`
- **title (draft):** Mind Your Feelings: a public art kiosk on mental health, built with TTN NY
- **summary (draft):** TTN NY helped design the electronics and firmware for Mind Your Feelings, a public art and community-health project by Juanli Carrión and Rodolfo Kusulas at the Johnson Public Library in Hackensack, NJ, and supported development of its tablet software. Visitors use an interactive kiosk and a lighted brain sculpture to name their feelings, see how they connect to the body, and find local mental-health resources.
- **tags:** Community, Health, Art
- **partners:** Northern NJ Community Foundation (ArtsBergen), Johnson Public Library, City of Hackensack Department of Health, Bergen Makerspace, Hackensack Stigma Free
- **funders:** Bergen County Division of Cultural & Historic Affairs (a partner of the NJ State Council on the Arts), Alfred N. Sanzari Family Foundation
- **dates:** opened December 11, 2025; on view December 2025 to March 2026
- **links.external:** https://mindyourfeelings.org

## 2. People counting in public spaces (NYC DOT, OTI, Meshed)

- **slug:** `nyc-people-counting`
- **title (draft):** Counting pedestrians in NYC public spaces over LoRaWAN
- **summary (draft):** From November 2025 to March 2026, TTN NY supported a trial of people counters for the NYC Department of Transportation, with the Office of Technology and Innovation. Meshed, an Australian company, put six solar-powered nCounters in public spaces in Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens. TTN NY set up and ran the four LoRaWAN gateways that carried the data.
- **tags:** Smart City, Mobility, Government
- **metrics:** sensors: 6; gateways: 4; partners: NYC DOT, NYC OTI, Meshed, MCCI (cellular backhaul), Voltaic (solar units)
- **sites:** Union Square Holiday Market, Madison Square Park, Fort Greene at Willoughby Avenue, Travers Park on 34th Avenue
- **links.external:** https://testbed.cityofnewyork.us/pedestrian-counting-in-public-spaces/
- **result:** more than 250,000 cumulative device counts at Union Square in one reporting period

## 3. Python classes for high-school students in western Kenya (WestLight Solar)

- **slug:** `westlight-kenya-python`
- **title (draft):** Internet and Python classes for three schools in western Kenya
- **summary (draft):** TTN NY is US fiscal sponsor for WestLight Solar, founded in Vihiga County, Kenya, by our late friend Bob Schneck. In summer 2026, gifts through TTN NY paid for Starlink internet at three high schools, so students could take live Python classes from volunteer teachers in the US.
- **tags:** Education, International, Community

- **metrics:** 3 schools; 200+ students trained (WestLight's report); Phase 2 target 30 schools, over 10,000 students
- **dates:** Starlink installed July 2026; classes began July 12, 2026
- **links.external:** https://www.westlightsolarkenya.com/

## 4. GLOwanus (Van Alen Institute, Brooklyn)

- **slug:** `glowanus`
- **title (draft):** Helping build GLOwanus lanterns for the Gowanus Canal
- **summary (draft):** GLOwanus is a public-art lantern from the Van Alen Institute that changes color when the Gowanus Canal is under a city water advisory, so neighbors know to use less water during storms. On September 16, 2023, TTN NY volunteers joined an assembly session at Van Alen in Brooklyn to help build the units.
- **tags:** Community, Environment, Art
- **partners:** Van Alen Institute (Neighborhood Design Fellowship); designers Manav Singla and Ridima Jain
- **dates:** assembly session 2023-09-16
- **links.external:** https://www.vanalen.org/project/glowanus/
- **image:** photo of the 2023-09-16 assembly session (used in the fall 2026 Mastodon post)
